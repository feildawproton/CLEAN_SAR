  A row where the bare int FAILS but c_size_t SUCCEEDS is direct evidence
  that the unprototyped size argument is being truncated at 32 bits.

  2**31 boundary = 2.0 GiB. Buffers in this codebase are
  H*W*8 bytes, so a scene larger than 268 Mpixels crosses it.
  Largest scene benchmarked here is 83.16 Mpixels (0.62 GiB) -- under the
  limit, which is why this has not yet been hit.

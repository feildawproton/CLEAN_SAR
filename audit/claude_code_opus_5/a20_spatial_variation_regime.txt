============================================================================================
WHERE THE FEATURE STARTS TO EARN ITS COST
============================================================================================
  scene half-width           R0 for >1% variation      R0 for >10% variation
  -------------------- -------------------------- --------------------------
     1.0 km                             R0 < 57 km                  R0 < 5 km
     2.5 km                            R0 < 143 km                 R0 < 13 km
     5.0 km                            R0 < 287 km                 R0 < 27 km
    10.0 km                            R0 < 573 km                 R0 < 53 km

  The 12 datasets audited sit at R0 = 647-764 km with half-widths of
  1.4-2.8 km, i.e. far outside every regime above -- hence 0.26 % of peak.

  The feature is real physics and correctly implemented. It becomes
  material for airborne collects (R0 of tens of km) and for very large
  scenes; at spaceborne ranges it is a sub-pixel, quarter-percent
  correction that costs a PSF recomputation on every CLEAN iteration.
